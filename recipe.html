Sausage Rice Casserole

This is a lovely and easy to make recipe that feeds six

Ingredients

  • 1 package of bulk sausage
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 1 packet of dry mix chicken noodle soup mix
  • 2 cups of white rice
  • 1/2 cup of water

Instructions

  1. first brown the sausage in a pan over medium heat
  2. when browned, drain excess fat and water from the pan
  3. steam the rice in a rice cooker until done
  4. chop the bell pepper into small squares
  5. mix all ingredients in a casserole dish mixing until the dry packet has disolved into the water
  6. place in an over at 350 degrees for 30 minutes
