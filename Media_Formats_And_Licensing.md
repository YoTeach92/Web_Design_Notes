## The Six License Types (Most to Least Permissive)

1. CC BY (Attribution)
	• What it allows: Share, remix, adapt, and build upon the work for any purpose, including commercial.
	• Requirement: Must give appropriate credit to the creator.
2. CC BY-SA (Attribution-ShareAlike)
	• What it allows: Share, remix, adapt, and build upon the work commercially.
	• Requirement: Must give credit and license new creations under the same or a compatible license (used by Wikipedia).
3. CC BY-ND (Attribution-NoDerivs)
	• What it allows: Copy and redistribute the work in unadapted form, commercially or non-commercially.
	• Requirement: Must give credit; no changes or derivatives are allowed.
4. CC BY-NC (Attribution-NonCommercial)
	• What it allows: Share, remix, adapt, and build upon the work.
	• Requirement: Must give credit; use must be strictly non-commercial.
5. CC BY-NC-SA (Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike)
	• What it allows: Share, remix, adapt, and build upon the work non-commercially.
	• Requirement: Must give credit and license derivative works under identical terms.
6. CC BY-NC-ND (Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivs)
	• What it allows: Download and share the original work.
	• Requirement: Most restrictive license; no commercial use and no modifications or adaptations allowed.
